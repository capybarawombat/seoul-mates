# Current playable loop

One shift has three alternating orders: tteokbokki, ramyeon, tteokbokki. The player chooses ingredients at a shelf, chops the recipe garnish, adds ingredients at the stove, adjusts heat, stirs by dragging in circles, plates at the counter and serves at the table. Temperature and doneness advance over time. High temperature, low mixing and overcooking reduce quality. Serving the matching dish pays once and moves to the next order. Finishing three orders enters a results phase; Enter begins the next day.

The next design milestone is a customer agent that enters, chooses a seat and exits, followed by an editable furniture grid. The original intent and longer roadmap are in `GAME_BLUEPRINT.md`.
